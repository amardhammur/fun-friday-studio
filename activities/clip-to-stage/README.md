# Clip to Stage

Show one reference clip, let every team rehearse, then bring each team to the stage. An offline judge panel decides the points; the host enters the totals into the app.

## Host setup

1. Add **Clip to Stage** to the event lineup and use 2–8 named teams.
2. Give the clip a title and choose singing, dance, or both.
3. Paste a YouTube video, Shorts, or clip link, or upload a playable MP4/WebM up to 100 MB. YouTube needs internet and loads only when you click **Load YouTube video**. YouTube clip links open in YouTube. Local videos play from this laptop and are saved in browser storage.
4. Choose the shared practice time and each team's performance time. Defaults are 10 minutes of practice and 3 minutes per team.

## Run the activity

- **Watch:** play the same clip for every team. Replay it as needed, then click **Start practice**.
- **Practise:** teams rehearse together. The reference stays available, and the practice timer can be paused or reset.
- **Perform:** click **Start first performance**, then **Next team** after each turn. Every event team performs once, in lineup order, with a fresh performance timer.
- **Judge:** after the final performance, click **Enter judge scores**. The panel discusses scores offline. Enter a nonnegative whole-point total for every team, including `0`, then **Save scores & see results**.
- **Results:** see the activity standings and move to the event leaderboard. **Edit scores** lets the host correct totals without adding duplicate points. Entered totals apply directly; the activity multiplier does not change them. Other activities and manual adjustments are preserved.

Timers never advance the activity automatically. **Pause** and **Resume activity** preserve remaining time; a timer you paused yourself stays paused. Keyboard shortcuts: `N` advances the current step, `T` starts or pauses a practice/performance timer. Shortcuts are ignored while editing fields.

## Offline playback and backups

Prepare the production app until its header says **Offline ready**, and upload a local video before disconnecting. Local clips, progress, and scores survive refresh and are included in **Export event** ZIPs. Importing a ZIP restores the video with fresh storage IDs. **Start over** resets this activity's progress and points while retaining the selected clip and settings. Clearing browser site data can remove the saved video, so keep a session ZIP backup.

YouTube playback requires an internet connection and may be restricted by the video owner or browser. If the player says **Video unavailable**, use **Open clip on YouTube** to play it in a separate tab or choose a different clip. **Retry player** reloads a player that failed to load. The app cannot override YouTube's playback restrictions. Upload a local video you already have for a fully offline event. Uploaded clips are retained if you replace the People library.
